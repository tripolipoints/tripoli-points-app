## Behaviour
- Each forward map step records the state before it (open/split clusters, selected shop, route on/off, exact centre and zoom):
  regional pin → split pins → cluster open → shop selected → route.
- Empty-map tap = exactly one step back, restoring that state and view: route → selected shop (card and walk/drive buttons kept)
  → open cluster → split pins → regional/overview. At the Tripoli overview it does nothing.
- Taps on pins, cards, buttons, lists, attribution or within 16 px of the route line are ignored by the step-back.
- «−»: at the zoom where a cluster/regional step was entered it goes back that step; otherwise a normal one-level zoom out.
  With a selected shop or route, «−» only zooms (never cancels). «+» unchanged (max 20).
- Pinch/zoom far out of an open cluster (more than 1.25 levels below where it opened) closes it, so a cluster pin never shows with
  its own shops. Switching walk ↔ drive is not a new step. Repeated taps add no duplicate steps; a shop tap within 0.6 s after a
  cluster opens is ignored (prevents a double tap selecting the shop that just appeared).
- Explicit controls unchanged: route ✕ (cancel route), card close (clearMapSel) and «Tripoli» (resetMapFilter) still do what they did
  and clear the step history.
- Changed in the app: the map's empty-tap handler calls tpnBgTap instead of clearMapSel; zoomMap is wrapped. No data, QR, points,
  account or Supabase change; same 68 database calls.

## Local tests (iPhone 13 viewport, outside network blocked, test data)
Forward 1–4 then back 4 taps: each restores exactly the previous step's state, centre and zoom; 5th tap at overview: no movement.
Tap on the route line: ignored. Walk → drive switch: no extra step. «−» sequence: normal zoom → split pins → overview → stays 13.
Double tap on split pin: opens the cluster only. Pinch out of open cluster: closes it. Zoom-20 same-building list: works; back deselects.
Home map: route → back keeps the shop and both route buttons. Login, merchant transaction (custKnown), controls 34×34: unchanged.
Known existing behaviour: a double tap anywhere on the map also zooms in one level (the app's double-tap zoom, unchanged).
